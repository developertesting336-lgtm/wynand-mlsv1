Pvverified Rentals

Operations Overview, Step-by-Step Workflows & Referral Flows

Project Overview

Pvverified is a premium real estate transactional portal that connects property owners, agents, and tenants (renters/buyers). It facilitates property submissions, local amenity mapping (via Google Places), document uploads, chat communication, lease agreement signatures, and moving reports in a unified layout.

User Roles & Signup

Users sign up by creating an account. During registration or on their profile, they choose one of the three primary client roles which determines their personal Dashboard environment:

  • Renter (Tenant): Accesses listings search, sends chat inquiries, uploads identity documents, signs leases, pays deposits/rent, and completes property inventory checklists.
  • Owner (Landlord): Adds properties, configures lease agreement templates, approves renter requests, manages active listing cards, and reviews moving reports.
  • Agent: Coordinates transactions, handles client chat inquiries, conducts move-in inspections, and receives property listings assigned by admins.

Additionally, the portal includes a dedicated Admin Dashboard where administrators review listings verification, inspect user uploads, approve/disapprove properties, track subscriptions, and assign agents to unmanaged listings.

Step-by-Step Core Workflows

Here is how a standard leasing transaction proceeds in Pvverified:

Tenant Account & Verification

After signing up as a Renter, the user navigates to their profile to upload mandatory verification documents: **Identity Document (ID)**, **Employment Verification**, and **Bank Statements**. Admins inspect these documents from the Admin Dashboard and change the profile's verification badge status to "Verified".

Property Search & Inquiry Chat

Renters search listings by location (Google Maps), view neighborhood amenities (restaurants, gyms, banks within 7km), and submit an **Inquiry**. This opens a real-time **Inquiry Chat channel** between the renter, owner, and assigned agent to coordinate pricing and viewings.

Booking Request Submission

Once ready, the renter submits a **Booking Request** on the property's availability calendar, specifying their desired move-in dates, budget, contact information, and lease notes.

Agreement Creation & Notification Flow

The agreement workflow progresses through sequential stages designed to keep all parties informed via automated system notifications:

  1. Drafting: The Property Owner (or assigned Agent) drafts the **Lease Agreement Conditions** (duration, monthly rent, security deposit amount, and documents requirements).
  2. Owner Init & Notification: The Owner signs the drafted agreement conditions first. The system automatically triggers a **sign request notification** alert to the Tenant.
  3. Tenant Review & Signature: The Tenant logs into their dashboard, reviews the conditions, uploads tenant document copies, and provides their digital signature. Upon signing, the system sends an automated **signature notification** to the assigned Agent.
  4. Agent Signature & Finalization: The Agent reviews the signed inputs, applies their finalizing signature, and the portal dynamically renders the official legally binding **Lease Agreement Contract PDF** stored securely on the cloud.

Move-In Inspection & Inventory Report

On move-in day, the Owner or Agent conducts an inventory check. They fill out the **Inspection Report** directly in the portal. This report records: * Detailed **inventory descriptions**. * Uploaded **inspection photos** showing the condition of the rooms. * Utility **meter readings** (electricity, water, gas). * Record of **keys issued**. The report is signed by the Owner, Tenant, and Agent, and saved as a verified PDF.

Move-Out Report

At the end of the tenancy, the same parties initiate a **Move-Out Report**. They note any damage, verify final utility meter readings, and sign off to confirm the return of keys and terms of security deposit return.

Automatic Payouts & Money Distribution Cron Job

Pvverified runs an automated background **payout distribution Edge Function** (triggered via a recurring cron schedule) to process all pending tenant rental and deposit transactions. Payouts are paid out directly to users' linked **Stripe Connect** accounts.

When a tenant completes a transaction, the funds are stored as a pending payment. The distribution logic calculates splits based on the lease duration, assigned agent, and referrer:

Payer Split Rules Matrix

  • Monthly Rent Payments: 100% of the rent is transferred directly to the Property Owner (Landlord) Connect account.
  • Initial Booking Payments & deposits:

    *Note: Initial booking fees, security deposits, last month's rent, and advance months payments are split to fund agent commission tiers before distributing the remaining balance to the landlord:*

    • Security Deposit, Last Month's Rent, & Advance Payments: These are processed together inside the initial booking total amount. The cron job calculates the total commission threshold based on the lease duration and caps the commission to the total initial payment amount if necessary. Any balance leftover in the initial payment after paying agent commissions (and associated platform fees + taxes) goes directly to the Owner.
    • Tiers: 12+ Months Lease = 100% of 1 month's rent. 6+ Months Lease = 50% of 1 month's rent. Under 6 months = 20% of 1 month's rent.
    • Case 1 (No Agent & No Referrer): Platform takes 10% platform fee + 16% IVA tax (recorded in platform earnings). The remaining 90% goes to the Owner.
    • Case 1.5 (No Agent, but Has Referral): The referrer receives a 15% share (minus platform tax). The Platform takes a 5% fee + 16% IVA. The remaining 80% goes to the Owner.
    • Case 2 (Agent Present, No Referrer): The Agent receives the calculated commission minus a 10% platform fee and 16% IVA. The Owner receives the total deposit minus the agent's commission.
    • Case 3 & 4 (Agent Present + Referrer/Referring Agent): The referring party receives 10% of the agent's net commission. The assigned Agent receives the remaining 90% of the net commission. The Owner receives the total deposit minus the commission.
Commission Calculation & Payer Splits (Example with $10,000 MXN Monthly Rent)

Step 1: Calculate Base Agent Commission
The base commission is computed relative to the lease contract duration parameters:
• 12+ Months Lease (100% of 1 month's rent): Base Commission = $10,000 MXN × 1.0 = $10,000 MXN
• 6 to 11 Months Lease (50% of 1 month's rent): Base Commission = $10,000 MXN × 0.5 = $5,000 MXN
• Under 6 Months Lease (20% of 1 month's rent): Base Commission = $10,000 MXN × 0.2 = $2,000 MXN

Step 2: Distribute Initial Payer Booking Amount ($25,000 MXN total initial payment consisting of $10,000 Security Deposit, $10,000 Rent, and $5,000 Advance/Last Month's Rent on a 12-Month Lease):

  • If Case 2 (Agent Present, No Referrer):
    • Base Agent Commission = $10,000.
    • Platform Service Fee (10% of commission) = $1,000.
    • IVA Tax (16% of platform fee) = $160.
    • Platform receives = $1,160.
    • assigned Agent receives (net commission) = $10,000 - $1,000 - $160 = $8,680.
    • Owner receives (rest of initial payment) = $25,000 - $10,000 = $15,000.
  • If Case 3 (Agent Present + Referrer):
    • Net Agent Commission = $8,680 (calculated above).
    • Referrer receives (10% of net commission) = $868.
    • assigned Agent receives (90% of net commission) = $7,812.
    • Owner receives = $25,000 - $10,000 = $15,000.
  • If Case 1 (No Agent, No Referrer):
    • Platform Service Fee (10% of total deposit payment) = $2,500.
    • IVA Tax (16% of platform fee) = $400.
    • Platform receives = $2,900.
    • Owner receives = $25,000 - $2,900 = $22,100.

The Edge Function connects to Stripe, triggers the transfers, logs the transaction IDs, and updates status flags from pending to paid in the database.

The Referral System

Pvverified features a two-tiered referral architecture designed to incentivize growth:

1. Agent Listing Referrals

Agents have custom referral codes. They generate URLs like https://domain.com/listings?agent_ref=AGENT_ID:

  • When a potential tenant visits using this link, the portal saves the agent_ref code in the client's browser storage.
  • When the tenant books a property, that agent is automatically assigned to coordinate the lease and collect commission.

2. Client Sale Referrals (Lead Generation)

Users can submit client leads (Buyer/Seller referrals) directly to Pvverified via the Refer and Earn page, which are stored in the database.

  • Automatic Booking Matching: When a booking request is made, if no active referral code is present in the session, the system searches the referral database matching the client's email.
  • Lead Priority: It prioritizes matching "Buyer" type referrals over "Seller" type referrals, returning the oldest valid match first to ensure the initial referrer is credited.
  • One-Time Commission Limitation: To prevent duplicate commission payouts on subsequent leases, if the client has any referral already applied (ref_applied set to true), no referral is credited. Once a referral payout succeeds, the system automatically flags that lead's ref_applied status as true.
Third-Party Integrations & Services

Pvverified leverages best-in-class third-party services to handle document verification, bank data extraction, automated contract compilation, and platform subscriptions:

  • Veriff (Identity Verification): Used to confirm renter identity documents (passports, driver's licenses) during verification. Veriff analyzes document legitimacy and cross-checks selfie photographs via a secure redirection session.
  • Belvo (Financial Bank Connection): Used to automatically retrieve bank statement logs. Tenants connect their bank accounts securely using Belvo API connections, eliminating manual PDF uploads.
  • Anvil (Automated PDF Contract Compilation): When an agreement is signed by the Tenant, Owner, and Agent, Pvverified invokes an Anvil function. Anvil programmatically signs, generates, and formats the legally binding contract PDF from the transaction conditions.
  • Stripe & Subscriptions: Used to process security deposit transactions, monthly rent payments, and platform subscription tiers. Users must subscribe to access search, listings, and messaging features.
  • Notifications & Service Workers: Inquiries, messaging chats, signature alerts, and verification status changes send real-time browser push notifications and emails to notify users immediately.
Core Technical Stack & Infrastructure

Pvverified is built using a modern, scalable serverless frontend and backend infrastructure:

  • Frontend (Vite + React): The user interface is built on **React** bundled using the **Vite** runtime environment for optimal build speed and fast page loads, styled using **Tailwind CSS** components.
  • Backend (Supabase PostgreSQL): Supabase serves as our primary relational database, storing schemas for users, profiles, bookings, listings, transactions, and audit logs.
  • Storage (Supabase Storage Buckets): Used to securely host uploaded user documents (ID verifications, employment contracts), property images/videos, and generated agreement PDF files.
  • Edge Functions (Supabase Serverless Functions): Serverless **Deno-based Edge Functions** are utilized to execute backend logic securely (like computing commission splits and invoking external services like Stripe Connect, Veriff, Belvo, and Anvil).
